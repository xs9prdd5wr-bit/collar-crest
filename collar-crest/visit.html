FAQ

The things visitors ask most. Anything else, ask the front desk.

Can I take photos?

Yes. Phones and cameras are welcome in every gallery. Please skip the flash near The Vault, where the oldest fabrics are most sensitive to light.

Can I touch the shirts?

During a handling session, yes, with gloves and a curator beside you. Everywhere else, glass stays between you and the polyester.

Can I wear my own kit?

Please do. Visitors in a club shirt get a sticker for the gallery that fits it best.

How do I see The Vault?

The Vault opens by appointment only, in groups of six. Book a viewing at the front desk or online.

Do you buy or authenticate shirts?

We don't. Donations go through curator review.

Is the building accessible?

Yes. Step-free entry from the loading dock, elevators to both floors, and seating in every gallery.
